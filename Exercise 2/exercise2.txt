Declare GPA
Declare SAT
Declare ACT

Display "Enter your GPA score, SAT score, and ACT score. If you didn't take the SAT or the ACT, enter a 0 as the score."
Input GPA
Input SAT
Input ACT

If GPA >= 3.5 AND (SAT >= 1200 OR ACT >= 25)
    Display "You are eligible."
Else
    Display "You are not eligible."

End If
